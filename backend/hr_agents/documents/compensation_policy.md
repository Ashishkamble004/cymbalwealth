# Cymbal Wealth Compensation & Benefits Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Issued by:** Human Resources — Total Rewards, Cymbal Wealth Financial Services
**Version:** 2.3

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines Cymbal Wealth's approach to compensation, salary reviews, variable pay, equity participation, and employee benefits. Our compensation philosophy is to attract, retain, and motivate top talent by offering market-competitive, performance-linked remuneration.

---

## 2. Salary Review Cycle

- Salary reviews are conducted **annually** in the **April cycle**.
- Revised compensation is **effective April 1** each year.
- Eligibility: Employees who have completed a minimum of **6 months** of service by March 31 are eligible for the annual review.
- New joiners are subject to the next full annual cycle unless their offer letter specifies otherwise.
- Performance rating from the Annual Review (March calibration) serves as the primary input for salary decisions.

---

## 3. Merit Increase Guidelines

| Performance Rating | Rating Description | Merit Increase Range |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | 12% – 15% |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | 8% – 11% |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | 4% – 7% |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | 0% |
| 1 | Below Expectations | 0% |

- Employees on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) are not eligible for merit increases during the PIP period.
- Merit increases are applied to **fixed base salary (CTC)** and are not retroactive.
- Total increase quantum per department is subject to budget approval by the CFO.

---

## 4. Variable Pay (Annual Bonus)

- Variable pay is **0–20% of annual CTC**, linked to both individual performance rating and business performance multiplier.
- Payout is made in **Q1 of the following financial year**, included with the **April salary**.
- Employees must be on the payroll as of March 31 to be eligible for variable pay.
- Employees who resign before payout date forfeit eligibility unless contractually guaranteed.

| Business Performance | Individual Rating 5 | Rating 4 | Rating 3 | Rating 1-2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exceptional Year | Up to 20% | Up to 15% | Up to 10% | 0% |
| Target Year | Up to 15% | Up to 12% | Up to 8% | 0% |
| Below Target Year | Up to 10% | Up to 8% | Up to 5% | 0% |

---

## 5. Employee Stock Options (ESOP)

- ESOP grants are available for employees at **Band 6 and above** (VP, SVP, EVP levels).
- **Vesting schedule:** 4-year vesting with a **1-year cliff**.
  - 25% vests after 12 months from grant date.
  - Remaining 75% vests quarterly over the subsequent 36 months.
- ESOP grants are reviewed annually as part of the compensation process.
- Exercise windows are communicated by the Finance & Legal teams.

---

## 6. Referral Bonus Program

Cymbal Wealth's Employee Referral Program rewards employees for successfully referring candidates who join the organization.

| Band of Hire | Referral Bonus |
|---|---|
| Bands 1–5 | ₹50,000 |
| Bands 6 and above | ₹1,00,000 |

- Bonus is paid after the referred employee completes **90 days** of continuous service.
- The referring employee must be employed at the time of payout.
- Referrals for roles in the same immediate team as the referrer are not eligible.

## 8. Employee Benefits

All regular full-time employees are entitled to the following benefits:

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Medical Insurance | ₹5,00,000 family floater (self, spouse, 2 children) with cashless hospitalization |
| Term Life Insurance | 3× annual CTC; designated nominee |
| National Pension System (NPS) | 10% of basic salary contributed by the company; enrolled from Day 1 |
| Mobile Allowance | As per grade; included in CTC structure |
| Internet Allowance | For roles with WFH flexibility; as per grade |
| Creche Benefit | Reimbursement up to ₹2,000/month for children below 6 years |

Additional benefits (wellness allowance, LTA, etc.) are detailed in the offer letter and vary by band.

---

## 9. Pay Date

- Salary is credited on the **last working day of the month**.
- In months with public holidays at month-end, payroll is processed one working day earlier.
- Payslips are available in the Workday HR portal by the 25th of each month.
