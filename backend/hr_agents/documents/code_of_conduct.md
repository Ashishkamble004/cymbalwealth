# Cymbal Wealth Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Issued by:** Compliance & Human Resources, Cymbal Wealth Financial Services
**Version:** 4.2

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Cymbal Wealth Financial Services is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, and ethical conduct. This Code of Conduct applies to **all employees, contractors, interns, and business partners** of Cymbal Wealth, regardless of role, location, or employment type.

Adherence to this Code is a condition of employment. Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

## 2. Customer Confidentiality

Cymbal Wealth operates under a position of deep trust with its clients. Accordingly:

- **Zero tolerance** for unauthorized sharing, accessing, or disclosing customer financial data, portfolio information, or personally identifiable information (PII).
- Customer data must be accessed only on a need-to-know basis for legitimate business purposes.
- Any accidental disclosure must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 2 hours of discovery.
- Employees who leave the organization must surrender all customer data and may not retain copies in any form.

Violations are treated as gross misconduct and are subject to immediate termination and potential legal proceedings under the **Information Technology Act, 2000** and applicable data protection regulations.

---

## 3. SEBI / RBI Regulatory Compliance

As a regulated financial services institution:

- All employees must complete the **annual SEBI/RBI compliance certification** administered by the Compliance team (mandatory — failure to complete by the deadline is a disciplinary matter).
- Employees must promptly report any regulatory queries, notices, or audits to the Compliance team.
- No employee may advise clients in ways inconsistent with SEBI-registered investment advisory norms without appropriate licensing.
- Transaction reporting obligations must be fulfilled accurately and on time.

---

## 4. Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

- Cymbal Wealth prohibits the giving or receiving of any bribe, kickback, or facilitation payment.
- Gifts from vendors, clients, or third parties with a value exceeding **₹1,000** must be declared to the Compliance team and returned or surrendered.
- Gifts below ₹1,000 must be modest, infrequent, and never given with intent to influence a business decision.
- Corporate hospitality (meals, events) is permissible when reasonable and transparent; excessive hospitality must be pre-approved by the department head.

---

## 5. Conflict of Interest

- All employees must **disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest** to their manager and HR in writing.
- This includes: personal financial relationships with clients, advisory roles outside Cymbal Wealth, directorships, and investments in entities that do business with the company.
- Employees must not participate in decisions or transactions where a personal conflict exists.
- Annual conflict-of-interest declarations are mandatory and must be completed by April 30 each year.

---

## 6. Social Media and Public Communications

Employees must exercise extreme caution on personal and professional social media platforms:

- Do **not** share client names, portfolio details, deal specifics, or any material non-public information.
- Do **not** reference or discuss unreleased products, pending regulatory applications, or financial performance before official announcements.
- Do **not** make statements that could be construed as official positions of Cymbal Wealth without prior approval from Corporate Communications.
- Media inquiries must be routed to the Communications team immediately.

---

## 7. Anti-Harassment and Respectful Workplace

Cymbal Wealth is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying.

- **Zero tolerance** for harassment of any kind — sexual, verbal, physical, or cyber.
- All complaints will be formally acknowledged within **24 hours** and investigated within **5 working days** by the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) constituted under the **POSH Act, 2013**.
- Complainants are protected from retaliation. Any retaliatory action is itself a disciplinable offense.
- Confidentiality of all parties is maintained throughout the investigation process.

---

## 8. Insider Trading

- The **absolute prohibition** on insider trading applies to all employees.
- Employees in possession of unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) relating to Cymbal Wealth or its listed clients must not trade in the relevant securities.
- **Pre-clearance** from the Compliance Officer is required before executing any personal securities trade during trading windows.
- Trading window restrictions are communicated by Compliance before each results period.
- Violations are reported to SEBI and subject to criminal prosecution under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

---

## 9. Whistleblower Protection

Cymbal Wealth encourages employees to report unethical behavior, regulatory violations, or concerns about financial impropriety without fear of retaliation.

- **Anonymous reporting:** Ethics Hotline **1800-XXX-XXXX** (available 24/7).
- Reports can also be submitted via the confidential ethics portal: ethics.cymbalwealth.com.
- All reports are reviewed by the Audit Committee and are treated with strict confidentiality.
- Whistleblowers are protected from adverse employment actions under the **Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014**.

---

## 10. Disciplinary Framework

Violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed through a progressive disciplinary framework:

| Stage | Action |
|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Verbal warning (documented in personnel file) |
| Stage 2 | Written warning |
| Stage 3 | Suspension without pay (pending investigation) |
| Stage 4 | Termination of employment |

Certain violations — including insider trading, data breaches, and sexual harassment — may result in immediate termination without prior stages, at the discretion of the HR Business Partner and Legal team.

---

## 11. Acknowledgment

All employees are required to sign and submit the **Code of Conduct Acknowledgment Form** upon joining and annually thereafter. Failure to submit the acknowledgment is a compliance matter.
