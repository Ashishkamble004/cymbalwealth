# Cymbal Wealth Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Issued by:** Finance & Human Resources, Cymbal Wealth Financial Services
**Version:** 3.0

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by Cymbal Wealth employees while performing their official duties. All expenditure must be necessary, reasonable, and in line with the limits prescribed herein.

---

## 2. Travel

### Domestic Air Travel
- All domestic travel must be booked in **Economy class**.
- Bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance through the approved travel management portal (MakeMyTrip for Business).
- Last-minute bookings exceeding 150% of the standard fare require additional approval from the department head.

### International Air Travel
- **Economy class** for flights of 4 hours or less.
- **Business class** for flights exceeding 4 hours (with prior approval from VP or above).
- Travel insurance is mandatory for all international trips and will be arranged centrally by the Travel Desk.

---

## 3. Accommodation

| Destination | Maximum Per Night |
|---|---|
| Domestic — Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune) | ₹8,000 |
| Domestic — Other cities | ₹5,000 |
| International | ₹15,000 (or equivalent in local currency) |

- Preferred hotel partners offer negotiated rates; employees are encouraged to use them.
- Exceeding the cap requires advance written approval and the excess is borne by the employee unless a business justification is accepted by Finance.

---

## 4. Meals & Daily Allowance (Per Diem)

- **Domestic travel:** ₹500 per day (covers all meals).
- **International travel:** ₹1,500 per day (or equivalent in local currency).
- Per diem is a flat allowance; no receipts required. Actual meal bills are not reimbursed separately.

---

## 5. Local Transportation

- Employees may claim actuals for local transport via **Ola or Uber** (official business account preferred).
- Cab/auto receipts are required for claims above ₹500.
- **Personal vehicles:** Reimbursement up to ₹2,000 per day at ₹12/km for two-wheelers and ₹16/km for four-wheelers. Claims above ₹2,000/day for personal vehicles will not be reimbursed.
- Metro/public transport actuals are reimbursable with valid tickets.

---

## 6. Client Entertainment

- Maximum reimbursable amount: **₹5,000 per person** (inclusive of all food, beverages, and venue charges).
- **Prior approval** from the reporting manager is mandatory before incurring client entertainment expenses.
- The claim must clearly state: client names, company, and business purpose.
- Alcohol expenses are reimbursable only for senior leadership (Band 6+) and when pre-approved.

---

## 7. Pre-Approval Requirements

Pre-approval is **mandatory** for:
- Any single expense item or booking exceeding **₹25,000**.
- International travel (all).
- Client entertainment.
- Training/conferences booked independently (outside L&D calendar).

Pre-approval must be obtained via the HR portal prior to committing to the expenditure.

---

## 8. Expense Submission & Receipts

- All expense claims must be submitted via the **Workday Expense Module** within **30 days** of the date of expense.
- **Late submissions will not be reimbursed** under any circumstances.
- Original receipts (digital or physical) are required for all items **≥ ₹500**.
- Claims without supporting receipts will be rejected by Finance.

---

## 9. Reimbursement Cycle

- Approved expense claims are **processed with the monthly payroll** (on the last working day of the month).
- Claims submitted and approved by the 15th of the month are included in the same month's payroll cycle.
- Claims approved after the 15th are processed in the following month's cycle.

---

## 10. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly **not** reimbursable:
- Personal travel add-ons (seat upgrades, lounge access for non-eligible employees).
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations.
- Personal telephone bills (beyond the mobile allowance in the compensation package).
- Expenses of accompanying family members.
- Gifts to employees (refer to Code of Conduct for gift policy).
