# Cymbal Wealth Performance Review Guide (Manager Edition)

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Issued by:** Human Resources — Talent & Performance, Cymbal Wealth Financial Services
**Version:** 3.1
**Audience:** People Managers (Band 3 and above)

---

## 1. Overview

This guide equips managers at Cymbal Wealth with the tools, timelines, and best practices to conduct fair, rigorous, and developmentally focused performance reviews. Effective performance management is critical to maintaining a high-performance culture aligned with our strategic financial goals.

---

## 2. Review Cycles

Cymbal Wealth conducts **two formal performance reviews per year**:

| Cycle | Focus | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-Year Review | Progress check, course correction, development conversations | October |
| Annual Review | Full-year performance, ratings, compensation recommendations | April |

Managers are expected to hold **informal 1:1 check-ins at least monthly** throughout the year to provide ongoing feedback. The formal review should not contain surprises.

---

## 3. Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals; recognized beyond team as a role model; drives significant business impact. |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Meets all goals and regularly exceeds several; proactively contributes beyond role scope. |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Reliably delivers against all established goals; competent and effective contributor. |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | Meets some goals but has clear gaps; improvement plan may be warranted. |
| 1 | Below Expectations | Fails to meet most goals; a Performance Improvement Plan is required. |

Ratings must be defensible with documented evidence. Avoid rating based on recency bias — consider the full review period.

---

## 4. OKR Framework

Performance is evaluated against **Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)** using the following split:

- **70% — Quantitative KPIs:** Revenue targets, portfolio performance, client retention, operational SLAs, compliance metrics.
- **30% — Behavioral Competencies:** Leadership, collaboration, customer obsession, innovation, regulatory adherence.

OKRs must be set at the beginning of each performance cycle in the Workday performance module. Retroactive goal-setting is not permitted.

---

## 5. Review Timeline (Annual Cycle — April)

| Week | Milestone | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (March) | **Self-assessment** submitted by employee via Workday | Employee |
| Week 2 | **Peer feedback** collected (minimum 3 peers + 2 skip-level) | Employee nominates; HR facilitates |
| Week 3 | **Manager review** drafted, incorporating self-assessment and peer input | Manager |
| Week 4 | **Calibration sessions** — department heads align ratings | Department Heads + HRBP |
| Week 5 | **Communication** — manager communicates ratings and feedback to employees | Manager |

All milestones are tracked in Workday. Managers who miss deadlines are escalated to their skip-level manager.

---

## 6. 360-Degree Feedback

Cymbal Wealth uses 360-degree feedback to provide a holistic view of performance:

- Employees nominate a minimum of **3 peers** and **2 skip-level** colleagues as reviewers.
- HR validates nominations to prevent gaming (e.g., nominating only close friends).
- Feedback is anonymized before being shared with the manager.
- Managers must incorporate peer and skip-level feedback meaningfully in the narrative section of the review.

---

## 7. Forced Distribution Guidelines

To maintain differentiation and calibration consistency, the following **guideline distribution** applies at the department level:

| Rating | Target Population Share |
|---|---|
| Rating 5 (Exceptional) | Maximum 15% of department headcount |
| Rating 4 (Exceeds) | Maximum 30% of department headcount |
| Rating 3 (Meets) | 40–50% of department headcount |
| Rating 1–2 (Below / Partial) | 5–10% of department headcount |

These are **guidelines**, not hard quotas. Significant deviations require written justification to the HRBP during calibration. Calibration sessions are the mechanism to resolve distribution anomalies across teams.

---

## 8. Calibration Sessions

Calibration ensures consistency and fairness across managers and teams:

- Chaired by the **Department Head**, with the HRBP as facilitator.
- All managers present their top and bottom quartile ratings with justification.
- Ratings may be adjusted up or down during calibration; managers must communicate adjustments sensitively.
- Ratings are locked in Workday only after calibration sign-off by the Department Head and HRBP.
- Calibration discussions are strictly confidential.

---

## 9. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

A PIP is triggered when an employee receives a **rating of 2 or below for two consecutive review cycles** (mid-year + annual, or two consecutive annual cycles).

**PIP Process:**
1. Manager drafts PIP with HRBP guidance — specific, measurable improvement goals over **90 days**.
2. PIP document must be countersigned by the employee, manager, and HRBP.
3. Weekly 1:1 check-ins are mandatory throughout the PIP period.
4. At PIP conclusion: if goals are met, employee returns to standard performance management; if not, escalation to termination proceedings begins.

PIPs are confidential. Managers must not disclose PIP status to peers or the broader team.

---

## 10. Rating Disputes

Employees who disagree with their final performance rating may appeal:

1. Submit a written appeal to the **skip-level manager** within **10 working days** of rating communication.
2. Skip-level manager reviews the appeal, consults with the HRBP, and provides a decision within 10 working days.
3. The skip-level manager's decision is final. No further appeals within the same cycle are permitted.
4. Appeals based solely on compensation impact (rather than factual inaccuracy) are generally not upheld.

---

## 11. Manager Responsibilities

As a manager, you are accountable for:

- Setting **clear, measurable OKRs** at the start of each cycle.
- Providing **regular, documented feedback** throughout the year.
- Completing all review milestones **on time**.
- Delivering ratings that are **fair, evidence-based, and free from bias**.
- Having **direct, empathetic conversations** with employees about their performance.
- Supporting employees who require development plans or coaching.

Managerial effectiveness in performance management is itself a KPI in your own annual review.
