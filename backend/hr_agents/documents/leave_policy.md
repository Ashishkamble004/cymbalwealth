# Cymbal Wealth Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2025
**Issued by:** Human Resources, Cymbal Wealth Financial Services
**Version:** 2.1

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a comprehensive framework governing employee leave entitlements at Cymbal Wealth Financial Services. It ensures fair and consistent treatment across all departments while maintaining operational continuity in our financial services environment.

---

## 2. Annual Leave

Permanent employees are entitled to **24 days of annual (earned) leave per calendar year**, accrued at a rate of **2 days per month** from the date of joining.

- Accrual begins on the first day of employment.
- Leave is credited at the start of each month.
- Employees who join mid-month receive a pro-rated credit for that month.
- Annual leave cannot be taken before it accrues; negative leave balances are not permitted.

---

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to **12 days of sick leave per calendar year**.

- Sick leave does **not** accumulate across calendar years; unused balances lapse on December 31.
- A self-declaration is sufficient for absences of up to 3 consecutive days.
- A **medical certificate from a registered practitioner** is mandatory for any absence exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- HR reserves the right to request medical documentation for frequent or pattern-based sick leave usage.

---

## 4. Maternity Leave

In accordance with the **Maternity Benefit Act, 1961** (amended 2017), female employees who have completed a minimum of 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months are entitled to:

- **26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave** for the first two children.
- 12 weeks for the third child onward.
- 12 weeks of adoption leave for mothers who adopt a child below 3 months of age.
- Work-from-home provisions for the period following maternity leave, where the nature of work permits, at the discretion of the department head and HR.

---

## 5. Paternity Leave

Male employees are entitled to **2 weeks (10 working days) of fully paid paternity leave**, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

---

## 6. Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to **3 working days of paid bereavement leave** upon the death of an immediate family member.

**Immediate family** is defined as: spouse, children, parents, parents-in-law, and siblings.

Extended bereavement considerations may be granted at the discretion of the HR Business Partner.

---

## 7. Emergency Leave

Employees may avail up to **2 days of emergency leave per calendar year**, subject to the following:

- Emergency leave is discretionary and granted at the sole discretion of the reporting manager and HR.
- It is intended for unforeseen personal circumstances not covered by other leave types.
- Emergency leave does not accumulate and cannot be encashed.

---

## 8. Public Holidays

Cymbal Wealth observes public holidays **as per the RBI holiday calendar for the banking sector**, which is published at the beginning of each calendar year.

Employees required to work on public holidays are entitled to a compensatory day off to be utilized within 30 days.

---

## 9. Leave Request Process

### Planned Leave
- Submit leave requests via the **HR Self-Service Portal** (Workday).
- Minimum advance notice: **2 weeks** for leave of 3 or more consecutive days.
- For leave less than 3 days, a minimum of **48 hours' notice** is expected.

### Approval Workflow
1. Submission via HR Portal triggers notification to **Team Lead**.
2. Team Lead must approve or reject within **2 working days**.
3. For leaves exceeding 5 days, approval is additionally required from the **HR Business Partner**.
4. Auto-escalation to skip-level manager if no action within 2 working days.

---

## 10. Leave Carry-Over

- A maximum of **10 unused annual leave days** may be carried over to the next calendar year.
- Carry-over balance **lapses on March 31** of the following year if not utilized.
- Employees are encouraged to plan leave utilization by February to avoid lapse.
- HR will notify employees of pending lapsing balances in January each year.

---

## 11. Leave Encashment

- Leave encashment is available at the time of **separation from employment** (resignation, retirement, or redundancy).
- A maximum of **30 days** of accumulated annual leave may be encashed at the time of separation.
- Encashment is calculated based on: **(Basic Salary / 26) × Number of Days Encashed**.
- Leave encashment is not available during the course of employment except under specific company schemes announced by Finance.

---

## 12. Policy Compliance

Non-compliance with this policy, including unauthorized absences, may result in disciplinary action as per the Cymbal Wealth Code of Conduct. All disputes arising under this policy shall be referred to the HR Business Partner for resolution.
